QRNG - Super Quantum Documentation

Overview

QRNG (Quantum Random Number Generator) - Super Quantum is a high-performance application designed to generate cryptographically secure random keys using true quantum entropy. Unlike standard pseudo-random number generators (PRNGs) found in most software, which rely on deterministic algorithms, our system sources randomness from physical quantum phenomena.

How It Works

Quantum Source: The application connects to the Australian National University (ANU) Quantum Random Number Generator API.

Vacuum Fluctuations: The ANU lab measures the quantum fluctuations of the vacuum. By splitting a beam of light and measuring the noise, they can generate truly random numbers based on the inherent unpredictability of quantum mechanics.

Python Proxy: To ensure security and handle API requests efficiently, a Python backend acts as a proxy between your browser and the ANU server.

Key Generation: The raw quantum data is processed based on your selected parameters (length, character set) to produce a final key.

Features

True Randomness: Sourced from quantum vacuum noise.

Customizable Keys: Adjust length (8-64 characters) and character sets (A-Z, 0-9, symbols).

Quantum Visualizer: Real-time representation of particle flux and qubit states.

Turbo Mode: Automatically generate new keys every 2 seconds.

Security: Keys are generated securely and history is stored locally in your browser only.
